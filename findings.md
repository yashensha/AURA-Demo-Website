# Findings - Zohaland Branding

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Zohaland
- **Core Value Proposition**: Customised Interior Solutions focusing on "Green Luxury", durability, and eco-friendly technology (Diatom Mud).
- **Tagline**: "Making Spaces More Elegant and Safer" (can be evolved).
- **Personality**: Professional, Tech-forward, Sustainable, Premium.

## Product Ecosystem
- **ZOHAWUD**: Diatom Mud Boards. Keywords: Air purification, Moisture regulation, 50-year durability, Stronger than PVC.
- **ZOHADECK**: WPC/ASA Flooring. Keywords: Weather resistant, Impact resistant, Elegant, Slip-resistant.
- **ZOHAWALL**: Wall solutions.

## Color Palette (Initial)
- **Primary Green 1**: `#47953F`
- **Primary Green 2**: `#2B7A34` (Forest/Nature feel)
- **Accent Earth**: `#301900` (Soil/Wood feel)
- **Neutral Base**: `#E3DFD7` (Linen/Sand feel)

## Typography Ideas
- Needs a balance of "Modern Tech" and "Natural Organic".
- Maybe a clean Sans-serif for headers and a readable Serif or premium Sans for body.

## Target Audience
- Villa owners, Hotel developers, Eco-conscious homeowners, Architects.
